Symbols of Divinity & Majesty

Inyambo Cattle & Regalia

In ancient Rwanda, cows were a symbol of prosperity, grace, and royal divinity. The Inyambo were raised with special songs (*Amatagisi*) and cared for meticulously.

Inyambo Sacred Cattle

Aristocratic Long-Horned Herds

Selected for their elegant long horns, regal stride, and serene temperament, Inyambo cattle were trained to march in rhythm during royal parades. They were adorned with elaborate woven headpieces (*Amashako*) and celebrated through royal poetry.

Symbols of Sovereignty
Kalinga (The Sacred Drum)

The supreme emblem of the kingdom's sovereignty. It was believed to hold the soul and unity of Rwanda.
